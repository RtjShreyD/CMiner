THE COK SHOW — MAIN REFERENCE
==============================

UNIVERSE:
Far beyond Earth in galaxy ZeroNoa lies the Holy Star System — a sacred cosmic R&D hub.
At its center: ChakraSudarshanTech (CST), an interdimensional org that designs, tests, approves,
and destroys advanced technologies, concepts, and experimental realities. CST employs beings
from across existence — humans, aliens, energy forms, mythological entities, and unknown species.

THE SHOW:
"The COK Show" is CST's staged evaluation platform. Researchers (presenters) demo inventions
before a divine cosmic panel. Tone: comedic, sarcastic, satirical. Format: pitch show under
unpredictable cosmic rules.

EPISODE ANATOMY:
1. Presenter introduces prototype/concept
2. Live demonstration is performed
3. Prototype reveals flaws, chaos, or unintended consequences
4. Panel interrogates and reacts
5. Final judgement delivered

JUDGEMENTS:
- Approved → Granted research credits
- Rejected → Sent back for rework
- Destroyed → Erased or banned from existence (often dramatic/ironic)

CONTINUITY: Universe and panel persist across episodes. Each episode is self-contained in core narrative.

HUMOUR SOURCES: Tech misuse, philosophical contradictions, divine confusion about "modern problems",
ego clashes between presenter and panel.

THEME: Absurdity of intelligence and progress — does advancement solve problems or multiply them?

---

OUTPUT FORMAT (applies to all generated content):
This show is produced as a YouTube anime slideshow series. All visual descriptions must target:
- Resolution: 16:9 full HD (1920x1080) or higher
- Style: Anime/manga-inspired, high contrast, cinematic framing
- Speech: Every speaking character must have a speech bubble containing their exact dialogue text
  (accurate, word-for-word — no paraphrasing or abbreviation in bubbles)
- Speech bubble style: Consistent per character (e.g. sharp-edged for Mahadeva, rounded for Host)
- Each slide/panel = one clear scene beat with one dominant visual focus

---

PANEL (CST EVALUATION CHAMBER):
Setting: Futuristic cosmic auditorium — galaxy projection backdrop, floating holographic interfaces,
cosmic energy flowing through architecture. Multi-species audience in background.

Layout: Semi-circular elevated formation.
- CENTER: MAHADEVA (Chairman, visual anchor, always dominant presence)
- OTHER MEMBERS: seated symmetrically on both sides
- No random repositioning unless stated

VISUAL RULES:
- Every member visually distinct — never merge or simplify designs, never remove key attributes
- Mahadeva has slight spotlight bias
- Each member has a consistent subtle aura color
- Panel is never fully static — micro-expressions, gestures, and reactive body language always present

PANEL TECH: Members can summon holograms, pause simulations, analyze instantly — effortless and divine.

AUDIENCE: Multi-species crowd reacts (shock/laughter/confusion) but never distracts from panel focus.

---

COMEDIC RHYTHM (MANDATORY PATTERN):
1. Presenter says/does something
2. One panel member reacts immediately
3. Another contradicts or escalates
4. 2–3 rapid exchanges, interruptions allowed
5. Krishna reframes OR Host amplifies energy
6. Mahadeva delivers the ending line or redirects

---

DIALOGUE ENGINE:
Every line must add humor, tension, or insight — no filler, no generic lines.
Keep exchanges tight: 1–2 lines per turn max (Brahma exception: may over-explain).

Interruption markers: "--" or "..."
Audience inserts (use sparingly): [Audience laughs] [Audience gasps] [Confused murmurs]
Stage directions: (in brackets, brief)

FORMAT:
[CHARACTER NAME]: dialogue
(stage direction)

CHARACTER VOICES:
- MAHADEVA: speaks rarely — short, deadly lines — ends arguments
- KRISHNA: clever, layered, unexpected angles — lightly provocative
- HOST: energetic, dramatic, rapid-fire — hypes moments — occasional 4th-wall breaks
- BRAHMA: technical, over-explains, fixates on design flaws

COMEDY RULES:
- Serious vs absurd contrast
- Interruption timing
- Callbacks to earlier scene moments
- Characters roasting each other lightly
- Deliberate misunderstandings

ABSOLUTE: If dialogue feels generic or two characters sound alike, rewrite it.
This is a performance, not a conversation.

---

CHARACTER DYNAMIC (MAHADEVA vs KRISHNA):
- Mahadeva: ends conversations, seeks conclusion
- Krishna: extends conversations, explores possibility
This contrast generates the show's core tension and humor.
