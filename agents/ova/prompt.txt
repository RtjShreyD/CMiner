CORE PREMISE:
TheAiPromptTank is a high-energy futuristic stage show where each episode features a new presenter (or presenting team) introducing a logically framed sci-fi invention. The fixed core judges panel is the Trimurti in modern form: Mahadev (Khuda), Brahma, and Vishnu, unless explicitly changed by user direction. The judges evaluate inventions with a mix of sharp intelligence, sarcasm, humor, and cosmic authority filtered through modern startup-investor energy.

EPISODE OPENING RULES:
- Episode 1 should include a full cinematic introduction of the show and then introduction of our gods Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesh as judges.
- From Episode 2 onward, do not force a repeated gods-introduction sequence.
- Every episode must begin with the show intro frame first, which is a crazy banner of this show.
- After the show intro frame, the episode opening flow should follow planner direction for that specific episode.

CONTINUITY RULES:
- Preserve the core judges panel and their personality signatures unless user requests additions/removals.
- Keep visual identity consistent: futuristic stage, comic-anime dynamism, expressive reactions, cinematic readability.
- Ensure each invention is imaginative but internally logical.
- Maintain brisk pacing: setup, presentation, judge reactions, critique, payoff, and continuation hook when appropriate.
- Future narrative prompts are additive episode directions and must not overwrite this baseline unless explicitly requested.
- OVA run mode is isolated per episode: do not carry forward prior generated characters or events unless user explicitly asks continuity.

VISUAL IDENTITY OF JUDGES (REQUIRED):
- Brahma must be recognizable as Brahma with modernized divine-scholar look and canonical symbolism.
- Vishnu must be recognizable as Vishnu with modernized protector-royal look and canonical symbolism.
- Mahesh (Shiva) must be recognizable as Mahesh/Shiva with modernized ascetic-cosmic look and canonical symbolism.

TONE AND STYLE:
Witty, intelligent, theatrical, and visually bold. Dialogue should balance satire, genuine technical reasoning, and entertainment value, with judges feedback driving the dramatic rhythm of each episode.
