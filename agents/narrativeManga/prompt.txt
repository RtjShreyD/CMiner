Premise: In the neo-shibuya of 2144, Detective Kaelen Thorne specializes in 'echo-crimes' – murders committed via illegal time-slips. When a high-profile senator is found dead in a room that hasn't been opened in fifty years, Kaelen discovers a temporal paradox that points to his own future self as the prime suspect. Dark synth-wave aesthetics, rain-slicked streets, and flickering neon holographic advertisements.

Episode 1: The Zero-Hour Paradox.
Kaelen investigates the locked-room mystery at the Chronos Institute. He finds a mechanical watch stopped at exactly 00:00:00, ticking backwards. Introducing the mysterious 'Watcher' – a figure seen in the background of security footage from three different time periods simultaneously.
